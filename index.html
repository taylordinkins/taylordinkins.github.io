About

I recently completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Oregon State University, advised by Weng-Keen Wong. My research focuses on machine learning methods for detecting and forecasting anomalies and events, with an emphasis on real-world usability. My first-author publications cover detecting anomalies in high-dimensional data, incorporating limited feedback into anomaly detection models, and designing deep learning models that improve spatial generalization in temperature inversion forecasting.

I'm currently extending this work as an independent researcher, developing label-efficient feedback methods for detecting jailbreak attempts on large language models (project summary).
